5. Objective 
**Theoretical component:**

  - The rationality assumption and efficient market hypothesis and know how they are violated in reality.
  - The systematic behavioral biases of market participants and its impact on asset pricing and corporate decisions.
  - Limits to arbitrage

**Practical component:**

  - How to code in Python and develop trading strategies to take advantage of mispricing. But more importantly how to design statistical test to find inefficiencies and taking advantage of inefficiencies.  



6. Themes of the course

- Introduction to Behavioral Finance & Decision-Making Under Uncertainty (mostly theory)
  The 3 big themes:
- Overreaction
- Underreaction
- Mispricing
